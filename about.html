OUR STORY

Our Story

Ananki Visviva did not start as a CNC company. It started as a group of people who loved to build things.

We were making home automation systems and robots. Interesting work. But every time we wanted to build something new, we ran into the same wall: we needed parts we could not buy, and components we had to make ourselves.

Making bespoke designs and parts by hand is slow and imprecise. Technologies like CNC machining and 3D printing change that completely - they let you take a design from a computer and cut it into reality, accurately, every time. We knew that was what we needed. The problem was, we could not find CNC machining capability readily available in Kenya. So we decided to build our own machine.

Jamleck Kabuba led the build. Dennis Kabuba, Pascal Magero, Cielo Whitney, and others helped. The machine was built from recycled materials - salvaged printer components, an Arduino microcontroller, and a lot of problem-solving. It worked. And it cut better than anything we could have done by hand.

That first machine was the proof. If we could build one from recycled parts, we could build better ones. And if we needed this capability and could not find it in Kenya, others did too.

In 2026, Ananki Visviva CNC Company has a fully equipped workshop and showroom along the Eastern Bypass in Ruai. We have built dozens of CNC machines for clients, run 5 in-house machines for production work, won a national innovation award, and trained hundreds of people. We are a Kenyan manufacturer looking to improve how manufacturing is done in Kenya.
