CNC SUPPORT

Keep Your Machine Making.

You imported a CNC machine from China and cannot get it running. Something broke after months of production. Or you are planning to buy a machine and need a clear answer first.

These situations happen every week in Kenya. Ananki Visviva has been building CNC machines from scratch since 2014. We know the components, controllers and failure points, so when something goes wrong, we know where to start.

WHAT WE OFFER

Practical Help For Every Stage.

Setup & Installation

We wire, configure, calibrate and test your CNC machine at your location. We leave when it is cutting correctly.

Machine Repair

We diagnose spindle, controller, axis, wiring and software faults. Common CNC spare parts are available locally.

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled service keeps your machine running longer. Quarterly and bi-annual maintenance contracts are available.

Pre-Purchase Consultation

Before you spend, we help you choose the right machine size, type and specifications for your actual work.

Remote Support

For straightforward problems, we guide you through troubleshooting by WhatsApp video call.
