BEGINNER LEVEL

Your Starting Point With CNC.

The Beginner Course is designed for people who are new to CNC and want to understand the technology before moving into more advanced work.

The goal is simple: help you understand the machine, the process and the basic skills needed to get started.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Start With The Fundamentals.

01

Understanding CNC

Learn what CNC means, how CNC machines work and where they are used.

02

Machine Basics

Get familiar with the main parts of a CNC machine and their roles.

03

Design To Production

Understand the basic journey from an idea or design to a finished product.

04

Materials

Learn about common materials used in CNC work and why material choice matters.

05

Basic CNC Operation

Develop an introduction to operating a CNC machine safely and correctly.

06

Practical Work

Apply what you learn through practical, hands-on exercises.

WHO IS THIS FOR?

Anyone Ready To Start.

  • Complete beginners
  • Students and trainees
  • Makers and hobbyists
  • Workshop owners exploring CNC
  • Anyone interested in learning a new skill
