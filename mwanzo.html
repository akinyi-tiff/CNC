MEET MWANZO

Your Starting Point For CNC.

Mwanzo is positioned as the small-machine option in the Ananki Visviva range.

It is aimed at people and organisations that want to get started with CNC without immediately moving into a large production machine.

Whether you're learning, experimenting, starting a small workshop or creating products for customers, this is where your CNC journey can begin.

WHO IS MWANZO FOR?

Built For Starting Out.

01

Home Hobbyists

For people who want to explore CNC, learn how it works and create their own projects.

02

Jua Kali Startups

For small businesses looking to add CNC capability to their workshop and expand what they can produce.

03

TVET & Training Institutions

For institutions that want learners to gain practical exposure to CNC technology.

WHY START HERE?

Start Your CNC Journey Without Starting Too Big.

You don't have to begin with an industrial machine to discover what CNC can do for your work.

Mwanzo gives beginners, small businesses and learning institutions a starting point for exploring CNC production.

And because it is built locally, you have a team nearby that understands the machine and the environment you are working in.

MACHINE DETAILS

Machine Specifications

Machine Type CNC Router (Wood, MDF, Acrylic, Aluminium, Soft Metals)
Effective Working Area 2400mm x 1200mm (4ft x 8ft full-sheet capacity)
Frame Heavy-duty solid steel frame, welded and reinforced
Drive System Rack and pinion (X and Y axes) + ball screw (Z axis)
Guide System Precision linear guide rails
Spindle 2.2kW - 3kW VFD Water-Cooled or Air-Cooled Spindle
Controller DSP Controller / Mach3 compatible
Software Compatible Artcam, Vectric Aspire, AutoCAD, DXF, G-code
File Formats G-code, DWG, DXF, STL, PDF
Power Single-phase or three-phase 220V / 380V
Materials Hardwood, MDF, Plywood, Acrylic, Soft Aluminium, Forex, PVC
Speed Up to 15,000 mm/min rapid travel speed
Included Machine + delivery + installation + 2-day operator training + 6-month technical support
