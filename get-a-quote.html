Tell Us About Your Project

You don't need to know everything about CNC. Just tell us what you have in mind. The more information you give us, the easier it is for us to understand what you need.

BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

What Should You Tell Us?

You can tell us about:

  • What you want to make
  • The material you would like to use
  • The size or approximate dimensions
  • How many pieces you need
  • Any design or finishing requirements

Have a design already?

Let us know. You can discuss your design with the team and find out what is possible.

We'll use the information you provide to understand your project and get back to you.
