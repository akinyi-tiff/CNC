INTERMEDIATE LEVEL

Move From Learning To Doing.

The Intermediate level is for learners who already understand the basics of CNC and want to develop stronger practical skills.

The focus is on building confidence, improving accuracy and understanding more of the CNC production process.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Build Stronger CNC Skills.

01

CNC Workflow

Develop a better understanding of the steps involved in taking a design through production.

02

Machine Setup

Learn more about preparing the machine and setting up a project before production.

03

Working With Designs

Improve your understanding of preparing designs for CNC production.

04

Cutting & Engraving

Explore practical CNC processes and how they can be applied to different projects.

05

Accuracy & Consistency

Understand how preparation and machine settings can affect the quality of finished work.

06

Practical Projects

Strengthen your skills through practical work and real project-based exercises.

WHO IS THIS FOR?

For Learners Ready For More.

  • People who have completed beginner CNC training
  • CNC machine users with basic experience
  • Workshop owners building CNC skills
  • Designers and makers with CNC experience
  • Learners who want more practical experience
